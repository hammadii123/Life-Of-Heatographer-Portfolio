## Name meaning (brand core)
**heart + photographer = heartographer.** Not "one who takes pictures" — "one who photographs with the heart."
Everything on the site should descend from this one idea: *dil se dekhna* — the street is full of people
nobody notices; he is the one who stops and notices.

## Colour grade (read directly off his photographs)
Highly consistent and genuinely his own:
- **Petrol/teal shadows** — deep desaturated blue-green in every shadow. The Karachi grade.
- **Turquoise accents** — painted benches, rickshaws, shop shutters (~#2AA9B0)
- **Ochre/turmeric highlights** — bananas, fries, chana, sunlit kurta (~#D9A441)
- **Warm tan skin tones** — held warm against the cool shadows
- **Sage-grey mids** (~#8FA39A) and an overcast Karachi white-grey sky
- **Night/rain:** near-black ink with magenta-blue wet-road reflections

→ Brand palette writes itself: **deep petrol ink ground + turquoise + ochre accent**, sage for secondary
text. This is not generic "teal & orange" — it is the exact grade of his own work, so the site frames the
photographs instead of fighting them.

## Typography direction
- **Urdu Nastaliq is non-negotiable** — he already overlays Nastaliq on his own photographs, and his
  captions lead in Urdu. Noto Nastaliq Urdu for the Urdu line of every story.
- Latin display: a high-contrast serif for the emotional register; grotesque/mono for metadata
  (date, place, shutter-speak) — evidence vs. emotion, the same split that worked on the Daleel site.
